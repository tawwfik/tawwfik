## About

**Tawfik Ahmmed** — Full-stack and mobile engineer in **Riyadh, Saudi Arabia**, shipping production software at [**Masar Soft**](https://msoft.sa).  
Computer Science graduate (**Dhamar University**, ~94% GPA). **English** and **Arabic**.

I build **Laravel** backends and **REST APIs**, **Flutter** apps (including **Saudi payment gateways**), and cloud-connected business systems — from discovery through release and operations. Focus areas: **SaaS architecture**, **ZATCA-aligned** accounting, **transport / logistics** integrations, and **domain-driven** design for the Saudi market.
